If you have spent any time in Roblox's anime RPG scene, you have probably heard of **Final Stand Remastered**. It is a reworked version of the long-running *Dragon Ball Z Final Stand*, and it has quietly built a sizable audience since its 2021 debut. This guide sticks strictly to what the official Roblox page documents, separates that from community chatter, and shows you how to check anything you are unsure about yourself.

## What Final Stand Remastered Actually Is

According to its official Roblox listing, [Final Stand Remastered](https://www.roblox.com/games/7922773201/Final-Stand-Remastered) is a remaster of *Dragon Ball Z Final Stand*. It is run by the Roblox group **Final Stand Remastered**, and the place was created on November 6, 2021. The page was last updated on October 12, 2026, which tells you the developers are still actively touching the experience rather than letting it sit.

The record also shows it supports up to 50 players per server and has accumulated roughly 19.7 million visits. Those two numbers matter more than they might seem. A 50-player cap is generous for a Roblox RPG, and 19.7 million visits over several years suggests a game with a persistent core audience rather than a flash-in-the-pan trend.

The official description also lists a full development team, including a lead designer, programmers, designers, modellers, artists, and an animator. That kind of credited structure is a decent signal of an organized project, especially in a space where many games are run by one or two people.

## The Dragon Ball Z Source Material

The developers themselves name *Dragon Ball Z* as the source material. That means the experience draws on the races, transformations, and general vocabulary of the anime — the same foundation the original *Final Stand* used.

It is worth being precise here, because this is where a lot of guides go wrong. The official page does not publish stat values, transformation multipliers, quest steps, or unlock levels. If you see a guide claiming a specific multiplier for a form, that is a community observation, not developer documentation. It might be correct, but treat it as second-hand and verify it in-game.

What we do know from the official description is the expansion structure. The current expansion is **Namek**, which places the game's content arc in a recognizable part of the Dragon Ball story.

For the bigger picture — expansion name, level cap, transfer cap, server size — the Roblox listing is authoritative and takes ten seconds to read.
