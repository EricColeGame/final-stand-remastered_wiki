That single sentence tells you most of what you need to know about the experience: it is a progression-driven game built around races, levels, and expansion-style content updates, and it has been running long enough to accumulate a substantial audience.

The official Roblox record is the authority here, and it is unusually specific on a few points. ## Races, Forms, and What the Source Material Supports

Dragon Ball Z is the source material the developers themselves name, so terms drawn from that universe, including its races and transformations, are fair to use when describing the game's theme. The credited wiki documents races and forms as part of the game's structure, and the official page's framing as an anime-inspired RPG is consistent with that.

What you should not expect from any authoritative source is a tidy table of stat values, multipliers, or unlock requirements. Those are exactly the details that change between updates, and the official page does not publish them. If you see a chart claiming a precise multiplier for a specific form, that is a third-party observation at best and possibly outdated. The honest way to check is in game: inspect the relevant menu or character screen after unlocking something, and confirm the numbers the game itself displays.

## The Namek Expansion and How Expansions Shape Play

Labeling Namek as the "current" expansion is a meaningful signal. It implies an expansion-based content model, where the world and its progression content are organized into named chapters rather than one undifferentiated map. For a player, that has two practical consequences.

First, new and returning players should expect that the most active content sits in the current expansion. Second, older content may still matter for leveling or for materials, but the developer's attention flows toward whatever is labeled current. The official page does not break Namek down into quests, zones, or rewards, so any step-by-step Namek walkthrough you encounter is community-authored. Those guides can be genuinely useful, but attribute them correctly in your own head: they are observations from players, not developer documentation.

## Level Cap 305 and Transfer Cap 125: Reading the Numbers

A level cap is the clearest kind of documented fact because it is a hard boundary the game enforces. If the official page says 305, then 305 is the ceiling until the developers say otherwise. The transfer cap of 125 is less self-explanatory. Because the official description does not define what a "transfer" is in this context, the careful interpretation is that the game contains some transfer mechanic with a limit of 125, and the details live in game rather than on the store page.

For planning purposes, the level cap tells you the scale of the grind, and the transfer cap tells you that some progression path is bounded. Neither number tells you how long either takes. Community guides often estimate hours per level band, but those estimates depend on training methods, group play, and update timing, so treat them as rough rather than reliable.

## Practical Advice for New and Returning Players

Approach Final Stand Remastered the way you would any long-running RPG with an expansion structure:

- Read the official page first. It is short, but it is the only source that speaks for the developers.
- Check the "last updated" date. The record shows October 12, 2026, which means the game is actively maintained. An actively updated game means community guides can go stale quickly.
- Verify mechanics in game rather than trusting wikis for exact numbers. Wikis are credited and often accurate about structure, but they are not developer documentation.
- Assume nothing about codes. The official page documents no promo-code system. If codes exist, they would be announced through developer channels, and you should confirm there before entering anything.
- Use the 50-player server limit to your advantage. Group up for content that benefits from numbers, and use quieter servers for focused training.

There is no guarantee that any particular approach will make you strong quickly, and anyone who promises that is selling something. What the documented facts do guarantee is a stable, actively maintained game with a defined expansion and a defined ceiling.
