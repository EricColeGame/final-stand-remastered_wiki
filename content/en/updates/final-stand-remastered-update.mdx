## Level Cap, Transfer Cap, and What They Imply

Two numbers do the most work for long-term planning: a level cap of 305 and a transfer cap of 125.

The level cap tells you the ceiling the current build advertises. If you're leveling, 305 is the number to plan around for this expansion cycle. Note that caps in Roblox RPGs tend to move when new expansions arrive, so check the official page again after any major update.

The transfer cap is the more interesting figure. In transfer-based Roblox RPGs, a "transfer" typically means carrying progress from one place or version to another, or moving a build into a new expansion or save. A transfer cap of 125 suggests that when progression carries over, only a portion of it is permitted, which is a common way developers protect balance between old and new content. The official page does not explain the mechanics behind it, so the safe reading is this: 125 is the advertised transfer limit, not a guaranteed conversion rate. If your plan depends on exactly how transfer works, confirm it in-game before committing dozens of hours to a build.
