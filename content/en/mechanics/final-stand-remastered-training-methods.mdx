## Level Cap 305 and Transfer Cap 125 Explained Carefully

Two numbers do the most work on the official page: level cap 305 and transfer cap 125.

A level cap of 305 sets the ceiling for character progression in the current build. That is a concrete, developer-stated number, which makes it the most reliable progression target available. It tells you the current expansion supports characters up to 305 and no further, at least as advertised.

The transfer cap of 125 is less self-explanatory, and the official page does not define it. In practice, a "transfer cap" usually refers to the highest level or value that can carry over between characters, servers, or versions of the game. Because the developer has not documented the mechanic, you should not assume a specific meaning. Verify it in-game: check the character creation or transfer screen, read any in-game text about carrying progress between slots, and confirm with other players who have used the feature. If you see a guide asserting a particular transfer rule with numbers, that is community interpretation, not an advertised guarantee.
