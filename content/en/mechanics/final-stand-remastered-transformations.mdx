## Understanding the level cap and transfer cap

The two numbers on the official page do different jobs, and players often blur them together.

The level cap of 305 is the ceiling for character progression in the current build. It reflects the Namek expansion being the active content tier. When the developers move to a new expansion, expect that number to change, because the official page is where they advertise it.

The transfer cap of 125 is a separate figure. The official description lists it without defining the mechanics in detail, so the honest answer is that the page states the number but does not spell out the full rules around it. If you need to know exactly how transfers work, check inside the game itself, read the official page again in case it has been expanded, or ask in a community space where players are actively discussing the current build. Do not assume a guide written months ago still describes it accurately.
