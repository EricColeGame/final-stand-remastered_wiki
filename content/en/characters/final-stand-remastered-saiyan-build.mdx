## The two numbers that matter most

Level cap 305 and transfer cap 125 are the only progression figures the developers put on the record, and they do different jobs.

The level cap is the ceiling on character growth in the current build. During the Namek expansion, that figure tells you how far the progression ladder is intended to run before the next content push raises it. Because expansions in this genre usually move the cap upward, a capped-out character is a snapshot of one content era, not a permanent endpoint.

The transfer cap is the more interesting one. In a game built around races, a transfer cap strongly implies that some mechanic lets a player move progress from one race to another, and that the mechanic has a limit. The official page does not explain how the transfer system works, what it costs, or what happens at the cap. It simply states the number. If you are weighing a race switch, read the in-game interface before committing, because the official page will not tell you the rules.

## Getting oriented as a new player

Pick a race based on what looks interesting rather than what a tier list claims, because tier lists for a game with an active update schedule go stale quickly and the official page publishes no balance data to check them against.
