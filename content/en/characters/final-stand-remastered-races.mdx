## Community Observations vs. Official Facts

Third-party guides for this game cover a lot of ground: race tier lists, form comparisons, grinding routes, and stat allocation advice. Some of it is genuinely useful. The problem is provenance. A guide author is describing their experience with a specific patch, and the same guide may sit unchanged for months.

Practical rules for reading community content:

1. **Check the date.** A guide written before the most recent update may describe outdated balance.
2. **Separate numbers from opinions.** "This race is strong" is an opinion. "This form gives a 2x boost" is a claim you should verify in game.
3. **Cross-reference the official page** for anything structural, like caps and current expansion.
4. **Test before committing.** If a build requires heavy investment, confirm the mechanic works as described on a fresh character or in a low-risk area.

This is especially true for anything involving drop rates, item prices, skill trees, or hotkeys. The official page does not document any of those, and the rules here do not permit inventing them. If a guide gives you an exact drop rate, that number is either datamined, estimated, or made up, and the source should say which.
