## What Final Stand Remastered Actually Is

The official description is refreshingly direct: Final Stand Remastered is a remaster of *Dragon Ball Z Final Stand*, and its current expansion is **Namek**. That single sentence tells you most of what you need to know about the game's identity. It is an anime-inspired RPG with the same bones as the original: pick a race, level up, unlock transformations and abilities, and push through content tied to the Dragon Ball Z storyline the developers explicitly name as their source material.

The "Remastered" label matters. This is not a sequel with a new engine and rebuilt systems from scratch. It is a remake of an existing RPG, which means players who know the original will recognize the structure, while the development team has been free to retune, rebuild, and reorganize content. The credited team includes a lead designer, programmers, designers, modellers, artists, and an animator, so this is a maintained project rather than an abandoned one.
