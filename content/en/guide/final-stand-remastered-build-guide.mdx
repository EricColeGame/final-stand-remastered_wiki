## Why the caps matter more than they look

Two numbers on the official page do most of the work in shaping how you play. The level cap of 305 tells you the ceiling of the current progression track. The transfer cap of 125 is the less obvious one. It implies that the game distinguishes between the progress you build on one character and what carries over when you transfer, and that the transferable portion is capped well below the maximum level.

Practically, that means a player who races to the level cap and a player who plans around the transfer cap are optimizing for different things. The first is chasing the top of the current track. The second is managing what survives a move. Because the official page does not explain the transfer mechanic in detail, you should treat any specific explanation you read elsewhere as a community interpretation rather than a rule. The reliable way to check is inside the game itself, where the relevant menus will show you your own numbers.

## Community observations versus documented facts

Third-party guides and Discord chatter are useful for orientation, but they are observations, not documentation. When a community guide describes a progression route, a preferred race, or a training approach, that is a player's account of what worked for them at a particular time. The official page does not document a promo-code system. It does not list item prices, skill trees, or drop rates. It does not publish a race tier list or stat multipliers. If a source presents any of those as developer-confirmed, that claim is not supported by the page we can actually verify. The honest position is: those systems may well exist in the game, but their details are not part of the official record, so specific numbers circulating online should be treated as secondhand until you confirm them yourself.

Here is how to verify anything you are unsure about:

- Open the game and read the interface directly. Level requirements, transfer options, and expansions are usually visible in menus.
- Check the official Roblox page for the current expansion name, level cap, and transfer cap before trusting an older guide.
- Note the last-updated date on the page. If a guide predates a significant update, its advice may describe a version of the game that no longer exists.
- Treat any specific number, price, or drop rate from a third-party source as unverified until the game shows it to you.
